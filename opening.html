Postdoctoral Fellow
This position is in the area of analog and mixed-signal IC design for biomedical applications, including analog-to-digital converters, instrumentation amplifiers, voltage/current references, CMOS/MEMS sensors and sensor interfaces. Tapeouts in advanced technologies are available.
Please send your CV and a brief research statement to Dr. Bo Wang for initial review.

Visiting Students & Researchers
We also welcome and provide financial support for visiting students and researchers to spend a period of time with us, typically 3–12 months, working on analog and mixed-signal IC projects together with our team. Visitors have access to our EDA tools, measurement facilities and tapeout opportunities.
Please send your CV, a research statement of no more than one page, and the proposed visiting period to Dr. Bo Wang for initial review.
