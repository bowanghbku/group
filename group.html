Principal Investigator

Dr. Bo Wang

Associate Professor | bwang@hbku.edu.qa

Dr. Bo Wang received the B.Eng. degree (Excellent Graduate Honor) in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Zhejiang University (ZJU), Hangzhou, China, in 2010; and the M.Phil. and Ph.D. degrees in Electronic and Computer Engineering from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), Hong Kong, in 2012 and 2015, respectively, under the supervision of Professor Amine Bermak, an IEEE Fellow. He was an Assistant Professor in the College of Science and Engineering, Hamad Bin Khalifa University (HBKU), Qatar, from 2017 to 2023, and is now an Associate Professor and Postgraduate Program Coordinator of the CElE Division.

In 2016, he worked in RLE, Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), with Professor Jing Kong and Professor Anantha P. Chandrakasan on low-power graphene-based pressure sensor interface design. From 2010 to 2015, he was with the Smart Sensory Integrated System Laboratory (s2is) of HKUST as a research assistant and then a research associate. From 2012 to 2014, he also worked as a mixed-signal IC designer at ZAMI, Hangzhou, to commercialize the chip during his Ph.D. research. He is a member of IEEE (CAS Society and SSCS). He received the Best Design Award at the Asia and South Pacific Design Automation Conference (ASP-DAC) in 2016. He served as the Technical Program Co-Chair of the International Conference on Microelectronics (ICM) in 2024. He also serves as a Technical Committee Member of the IEEE CAS Technical Committee on Sensory Systems and is an Associate Editor for the IEEE Sensors Journal.
